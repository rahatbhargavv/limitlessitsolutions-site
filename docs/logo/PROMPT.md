# Logo design prompt: Limitless IT Solutions

## Role and objective
You are a senior brand identity designer. Design a logo for **Limitless IT Solutions**, a small, owner-run IT support business serving homes and small businesses in Ontario, Canada. The logo is used on the website header, favicon, email signature, invoices and social share image. It must signal calm competence, trust and quiet strength, the way a private bank or an expedition outfitter would, not a flashy tech start-up.

## Reference (study, never copy)
The reference is the Montfort Group mark (mont-fort.com). Extract these qualities:
- A **stippled emblem**: a symbol built entirely from small round dots of varying size, so it reads as organic and technological at once.
- **One colour only** (a calm, deep, desaturated blue), flat. No gradients, glows, bevels, outlines or shadows.
- A **wordmark in ultra-light geometric capitals**, very wide letter-spacing, set beside the emblem and separated from it by a thin vertical hairline.
- Huge amounts of negative space; nothing decorative.
Do not reproduce Montfort's tree/heart shape or its exact lettering. The result must be unmistakably original.

## Concept
- Emblem: a **stippled, bilaterally symmetric eagle in flight, wings raised** (the brave, authentic character the owner asked for, in the spirit of heraldic eagles) formed only from dots. Dots are largest and densest at the chest and shrink toward the wing tips and tail until they dissolve into nothing. This dissolving edge is the visual idea of "limitless". The silhouette must be readable at 48 px.
- Wordmark: **LIMITLESS** in light geometric caps, with **IT SOLUTIONS** beneath in much smaller type at the same tracking style.
- Layout: emblem on the left, 1 px vertical divider, wordmark on the right (horizontal lockup). Also supply a stacked version and an emblem-only square.

## Constraints
- Palette: single ink colour deep teal `#14524F` on white; reversed version in white on `#0B1F1E`. No second colour.
- Flat vector SVG, no raster effects, no live fonts (text converted to outlines).
- Must work in one colour, at 32 px (favicon), and on both light and dark backgrounds.
- Avoid: clip-art feel, thick outlines, gold or glow effects, shields, circuit-board cliches, more than ~400 dots in the emblem.

## Output
1. `limitless-logo-horizontal.svg`, `limitless-logo-stacked.svg`, `limitless-mark.svg` (one colour).
2. Reversed (dark-background) versions of each.
3. A preview sheet PNG showing: horizontal lockup on white, on dark, and the mark at 256 / 64 / 32 px.

## Self-check before delivering
Does it still look refined when shrunk to 32 px? Is it clearly an eagle? Is it one flat colour? Would it look at home next to Montfort's mark without being a copy? If any answer is no, revise.
