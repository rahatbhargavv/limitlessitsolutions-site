# Google Business Profile setup guide

Create at business.google.com. Choose **service-area business** and hide your home address (you have no public address).

- **Business name:** Limitless IT Solutions (exactly, no keywords added; Google suspends keyword stuffing).
- **Primary category:** Computer support and services. **Additional:** Wi-Fi network installation (if offered in your region), Security system installer, Computer repair service, Computer consultant. Check the category list in the dashboard; names change.
- **Service areas:** Toronto, then nearby cities you really serve. Google allows up to 20 areas.
- **Phone / website:** your tracked number and https://limitlessitsolutions.ca
- **Hours:** [HOURS]. Do not claim 24/7.
- **Services to list:** Wi-Fi and network setup; Network troubleshooting; Security camera installation; Computer setup; Computer repair; Data transfer; Annual Tech Checkup.
- **Description (under 750 characters):** "Limitless IT Solutions is a new, owner-operated IT service for homes and small businesses in Toronto, Ontario. We help with Wi-Fi and network setup and fixes, security camera installation with phone viewing, and computer setup, repair and data transfer. Plain language, tidy work and honest quotes. Request a quote on our website or call."
- **Verification:** Google will ask for video or phone/email verification. Follow its prompts honestly.

## Photo shot list (your own photos only)
1. Logo. 2. A tidy router/access point install. 3. Neat cable run. 4. A camera mounted on a house or shop (get owner permission). 5. A phone showing the camera app (no private footage). 6. Your tools/van, if any. 7. A before/after of a tidy cabinet.

## Getting your review link
1. Open your profile on Google (search your business name while signed in).
2. Click **Ask for reviews** (or **Get more reviews**).
3. Copy the short link. Save it in `site.config.mjs` area as [GBP REVIEW LINK] and use it below.

## Review-request text (send after the job, one customer at a time, only to customers who agreed to be contacted)
> Hi [Name], thanks for choosing Limitless IT Solutions. If you were happy with the work, a short Google review would really help a new local business: [GBP REVIEW LINK]. Thank you!

Rules: never offer rewards for reviews, never write reviews yourself, never gate reviews to only happy customers.
